Ongaku for Spotify Setup Guide

Note: Spotify Premium is required.

Step 1: Create a Spotify App

1. Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  • Important: Open the dev dashboard in Safari/Chrome/desktop, not inside the Pebble app.
  • If using mobile browser: You may need to set your browser zoom to 50% to see the "Create App" button.
2. Click Create App.
3. Fill in the details:
  • App name: anything you like (e.g. "My Ongaku")
  • App description: anything (e.g. "Pebble music control")
  • Redirect URI: paste the URL below exactly:
https://ongaku.yoiailabs.com/oauth/callback
4. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?, select Web API.
5. Check the Terms of Service box and click Save.

If you used the previous configuration page, add this new Redirect URI to your existing Spotify app before logging in again.

Step 2: Copy Your Client ID

6. On your new app's page, click Settings.
7. Copy the Client ID shown at the top. It looks like: a1b2c3d4e5f6...
Important: You do NOT need the Client Secret. Ongaku for Spotify uses PKCE authentication which doesn't require a secret.

Step 3: Enter It in Ongaku for Spotify

8. Open the Ongaku for Spotify settings on your phone (Pebble app → Ongaku for Spotify → Settings).
9. Paste your Client ID into the Spotify Client ID field at the top of the login page.
10. Tap Log in with Spotify and authorize the app.

That's it!

Your Pebble will now connect to Spotify using your own app credentials. Spotify account and developer app restrictions still apply.
